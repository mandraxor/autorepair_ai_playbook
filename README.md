## 🚀 Key Interactive AI Tools Included

1. **AI Diagnostic & DTC Fault Code Triage Engine (OBD-II)**
   - Resolves complex trouble codes (P0300, P0420, P0012, C1201, EV & Diesel codes).
   - Generates component failure probabilities, oscilloscope/multimeter bench test steps, OEM TSB cross-checks, and estimated diagnostic book hours.

2. **Smart Repair Order (RO) & Parts Margin Optimizer**
   - Instant parts & labor estimate breakdown with OEM vs Aftermarket pricing tiers.
   - Real-time margin calculations, wholesale vs list markup, and "Good / Better / Best" packages.

3. **"Tech-to-Customer" Plain-English Translator & SMS Approver**
   - Converts dense mechanic jargon into customer-friendly, trust-building explanations with urgency scores.
   - Live iPhone preview with simulated 1-click customer SMS approval flow.

4. **Computer Vision Wear & Damage Inspector**
   - Interactive visual inspection simulator for brake pads, tire tread wear, belts, and fluid leaks.
   - Generates digital Multi-Point Inspection (MPI) cards with bounding boxes and technician annotations.

5. **AI Bay & Technician Labor Optimization Matrix**
   - Shop floor load balancer matching job difficulty to Master Tech vs Apprentice certifications.
   - Real-time calculation of shop efficiency rating and billable labor throughput.

6. **Automotive Aftermarket AI Prompt & Automation Library**
   - High-converting prompts and Zapier/Make webhook blueprints for Tekmetric, Shopmonkey, Mitchell 1, and Shop-Ware.

7. **Shop ROI & Profit Lift Calculator**
   - Dynamic financial modeling based on bay count, ARO (Average Repair Order), and labor rate.
   - Live Chart.js visualization of before-and-after net profit expansion.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Integrations
